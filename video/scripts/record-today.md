## Kate: the chairs (about 50 seconds)

> I picked the chairs. On Thursday there's no decision to make. You just carry twenty-four chairs up six flights of stairs, and how many people help depends on how you treated them earlier in the week.
>
> That one got me, because when I led a group project, people were busy and missing meetings, and I wanted to be kind, so I quietly took their work on myself. Eventually a teammate noticed and told me, really gently, to stop carrying it all.
>
> The game has the same moment. Do everything yourself, and you're alone on the stairs, and later someone writes, "You could have asked. I'd have carried chairs."
>
> So I still think kindness comes back to you. But being kind also means letting people carry it with you.
