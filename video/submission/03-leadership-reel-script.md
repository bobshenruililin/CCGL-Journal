# Leadership trailer: script and storyboard

40 seconds, vertical 1080×1920 for Instagram or Reels; the first 12 seconds also work as the film's cold open (in 16:9).

**The idea:** the viewer is the leader. The trailer asks them to decide, then shows what each choice costs, through the five lessons the four of us drew from the course. AI isn't mentioned until the small credit at the end.

The music is the same code-made 120 BPM track: one beat is 0.5 s.
