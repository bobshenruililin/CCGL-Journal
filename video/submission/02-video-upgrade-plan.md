# Making the final video captivating: an honest review and a weekend plan

Current cut: 5:46. Samantha has said "five minutes and something" is fine, so the budget is about **5:59**. That leaves **13 seconds** to add.

## Honest review of the current cut

**What already works**
- Each section has one person, one idea and one lesson. The structure is clear.
- The personal stories (Bob's friends, Kate's group project, Hannah's debate, Amanda's dance team) are the most moving moments.
- The growing lesson list makes progress visible.
- The opening and the ending look designed, not templated.

**What holds it back**
1. **The first 10 seconds ask for patience.** It opens on a voiceover over animation. A marker decides early whether this will be good, and right now nothing *happens* to them.
2. **Bob's opening read is flat**, as you said. Energy at the start sets the energy for the whole video.
3. **Every section has the same layout and the same rhythm** for five minutes. By the fourth section the eye knows what's coming.
4. **The middle is silent between words.** No music bed, so pauses feel empty rather than reflective.
5. **The game footage is mostly still pages with slow zooms.** The painted, moving version we made for the trailer is far more alive.
6. **The leadership ideas are said but not shown as a pattern.** A viewer hears five lessons but doesn't *see* that they add up.
