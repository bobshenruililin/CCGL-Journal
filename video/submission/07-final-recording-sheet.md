# Bob: final recording sheet

Five files. Record each as its own take, two takes each, phone close, quiet room, standing. **Bold** = lean on it. `/` = short pause.

Two parts changed since `04-new-cut-and-scripts.md`: the opening (it now hands off from the Moodle journal) and the first line of Saturday. The other three are unchanged.

---

### 1. `bob-intro-v4`: opening (about 14 s) **NEW**

It plays right after the Moodle scroll, so it starts from the journal.

> **Hi!** We're four students from **CCGL9079**, Leadership and Influence. /
> Every week, this course asks us to write in a journal: / what is **your** definition of leadership, / and do you **fit** it? /
> Writing the answer is **easy.** / **Living** it is harder. /
> So we built a game… where you **have to.**

### 2. `bob-monday-v2`: Monday (about 35 s), unchanged

> It's called **Six Flights.** You're Yan, and you've just inherited a rooftop film night. / **One week. Three decisions.** /
> The first one is about **Kit.** He runs the projector, and he's ignored every message for **five days.** / You can work around him… / or go downstairs and **knock.** /
> I put that first because I've had friends go quiet, and everyone assumed they'd **stopped caring.** / It was family. A breakup. Things **none of us could see.** /
> Knock, and you find out what's really going on, and it changes the **whole week.** / So: **ask before you assume.**

### 3. `bob-saturday-v3`: Saturday (about 34 s), **new first line**

> **Most games about leadership score you.** / Six Flights shows you **who paid.** /
> On Saturday, your teammates take the notebook and **write in it themselves**, / and that's your only feedback: / what your choices were **like for them.** /
> There are **twenty-seven** different weeks, and I hope you play **more than one.** / Someone once told me to **increase my surface area**: / try more things, because that's how you find **what fits.** /
> There's no right answer here. / But **every choice lands on somebody.**

### 4. `bob-projects-v2`: three builds (about 42 s), unchanged

> Six Flights wasn't our **first try.** / Kate built **Team Quest**, which scored your **rapport** and **energy.** / It taught us that leading means **spending trust**, / but a score can't show you **who's paying** for your choices. /
> So we built Six Flights. / Then we pushed further with **Asterwind**, where you're **in the room.** / You make the call, / then you have to **say it** to the person it costs, / in a sentence you **build yourself.** /
> It still costs her. / But she knows she was **part of the decision.**

### 5. `bob-closing`: last line (about 5 s), unchanged

> **Three games.** / **One question.** / What is it like… to be **led by you?**

## Tips
- Leave one second of silence before and after each take. It makes the cuts clean.
- On the opening, "**Hi!**" can be big; the rest is conversational. Smile on "where you **have to.**"
- If a line trips you up, just say it again in the same take. I'll cut the better one.
- Send the files as they are (m4a, wav or mp3 all work).
