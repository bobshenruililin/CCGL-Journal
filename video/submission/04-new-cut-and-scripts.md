# The new cut: running order, new ideas, and Bob's scripts

Bob currently speaks for about 3 minutes of the 5:46. Tightening his four parts to about 2:10 frees roughly 50 seconds. That time goes into moments that **show** the work instead of describing it. Target length is about **5:45–5:58**.

## New ideas, beyond items 1–5

| Idea | What the viewer sees | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A. Two Mondays, side by side** | Bob's section splits the screen: on the left you knock and the door opens; on the right you post in the group chat and "Kit left the group". | The whole game in one image: the same week, two choices, two people. |
| **B. Each speaker steps into their scene** | For two seconds at the start of each section, the speaker's cut-out appears *inside* the painting: Amanda in the rooftop audience, Hannah on the stormy roof, Kate on the stairs, Bob at Kit's door. | Personal, and it ties each of us to the part we chose. |
| **C. Page-turn transitions** | Each section ends with a 3D page turn, like the game, revealing the next day's tab. | The film becomes the notebook. On-brand, and it breaks the identical rhythm. |
| **D. "By the numbers" (6 s, before part two)** | Numbers slam in on the beat: *27 weeks · 4 endings · 6,000 words · 300+ paintings reviewed · 14 briefs · 3 builds · 1 question.* | Makes the amount of work evident in six seconds. |
| **E. The cutting-room floor (4 s, in Bob's projects part)** | Rejected paintings, our written briefs and the waveform flick past like a contact sheet. | Shows iteration: we threw away more than we kept. |
| **F. The five lessons, in our own voices** | At the end, each person's lesson line plays back to back in their own voice, while the line writes itself into Yan's notebook. | The emotional close. It reuses recordings we already have; nobody needs to re-record. |
| **G. Key words highlighted in subtitles** | The lesson words in each person's subtitles turn yellow as they're said. | Helps a skimming marker catch the concepts. |

**Recommended set: A, C, D, F and G**, plus items 1–5 from the upgrade plan. B and E are nice-to-haves if time allows.

## Bob: what to record

Record each part as its own file, standing, phone close, quiet room, two takes each. **Bold** means lean on it. A slash means a short pause. Energy up, but keep it conversational.

### 1. `bob-intro-v3`: opening (about 11 s)

> **Hi!** We're four students from **CCGL9079**, Leadership and Influence. / All semester, **one question** kept coming back to us: / how do you let someone **practise** leading, / **before it's for real?** / Our answer… was to **build a game.**

### 2. `bob-monday-v2`: Monday (about 35 s)

> It's called **Six Flights.** You're Yan, and you've just inherited a rooftop film night. / **One week. Three decisions.** /
> The first one is about **Kit.** He runs the projector, and he's ignored every message for **five days.** / You can work around him… / or go downstairs and **knock.** /
> I put that first because I've had friends go quiet, and everyone assumed they'd **stopped caring.** / It was family. A breakup. Things **none of us could see.** /
> Knock, and you find out what's really going on, and it changes the **whole week.** / So: **ask before you assume.**

### 3. `bob-saturday-v2`: Saturday (about 32 s)

> **Six Flights never gives you a score.** / On Saturday, your teammates take the notebook and **write in it themselves**, / and that's your only feedback: / what your choices were **like for them.** /
> There are **twenty-seven** different weeks, and I hope you play **more than one.** / Someone once told me to **increase my surface area**: / try more things, because that's how you find **what fits.** /
> There's no right answer here. / But **every choice lands on somebody.**

### 4. `bob-projects-v2`: three builds (about 42 s)

> Six Flights wasn't our **first try.** / Kate built **Team Quest**, which scored your **rapport** and **energy.** / It taught us that leading means **spending trust**, / but a score can't show you **who's paying** for your choices. /
> So we built Six Flights. / Then we pushed further with **Asterwind**, where you're **in the room.** / You make the call, / then you have to **say it** to the person it costs, / in a sentence you **build yourself.** /
> It still costs her. / But she knows she was **part of the decision.**

### 5. `bob-closing`: last line (about 5 s)

> **Three games.** / **One question.** / What is it like… to be **led by you?**
