## The insight the film is built on

The course gives every student the same journal question: **"Do you fit your definition of leadership?"** You can't answer that alone. Only the people you lead can. That's exactly what Six Flights does on Saturday, when your teammates take your notebook and write in it themselves.

So the film opens on the real Course Journal page in Moodle and ends on the game's own last page: *"There is one line left on this page. It's yours."* The marker reads their own question at the start and our answer at the end.
