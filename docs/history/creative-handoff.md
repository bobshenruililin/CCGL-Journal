## For Opus: one exemplar, not broad discovery

Bob has limited Opus credits. No Opus session has been contacted by this build. Establish a front-end standard using one exceptionally crafted interaction, then give reproducible rules. Start from app/page.tsx, app/globals.css, app/PixelRoom.tsx and app/AfterRain.tsx.

Desired moment: the reader chooses one of three dotted handwritten lines. The line becomes ink, retaining immediate visible selection and an accessible announcement. A later page preserves that mark as a meaningful memory. A character beat—not a correct-answer score—can release the restrained palette into colour. Motion should communicate the act of writing, noticing, or turning a page; no forced delay or constant confetti.

Deliver: (1) a working chosen-line exemplar; (2) typography, paper, colour, motion and focus rules; (3) two tension-to-release transition examples; (4) reduced-motion and mobile equivalents. Avoid another generic dashboard, quiz cards, and decorative pseudo-personality meters. Compare actual screenshots. Use existing fonts and original artwork unless new assets have verified licenses.
