# Uncoached playtest, 12 November 2026: model reviewer

**What this is.** A separate model agent was given only the running page and told not to read the repository. It drove Chromium with real clicks and taps, looking at screenshots to decide what to do: one full route on desktop (1366×800), one different route on a phone (390×844), then the replay and rewrite paths. It answered eight questions from memory before its second play.

**What this is not.** A person. It cannot tell us how the piece feels to hold, whether the sound helps, or how long a human lingers. Request 05 (people who have never seen it) is still open.

Build tested: `2fb11fa`.

## What it understood, unprompted

- The situation, all five people and one true thing about each.
- Three consequences of knocking on Monday (the missed licence form, the vent, "Thanks for knocking") and one of going round on Wednesday (the untested speaker).
- Lines it quoted as landing: "every message he didn't open made the next one heavier", "Thanks for knocking", Kit's "(I have $20. it's yours.)", Mrs Leung's kept chair.

## What it did not understand

- **The colour.** With everyone lit on its first play it read the pools as "pretty lighting". It only saw the meaning on the second play, when Yan was alone in colour. Its words: "the point only shows when you compare the two endings".
- **Own words.** "Leave noodles outside his door with a note" was filed under "Go and knock" with no acknowledgement: "it felt like being sorted into a bin".

## Problems it found, and what was done

| Finding | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Colour carried no meaning on a first play | Each person in colour now has a few handwritten words beside them naming what was theirs that night |
| Typed words sorted silently | The page now says "which, written down, comes to:" before the authored line; the doubtful case says it cannot tell |
| A consequence line cut off at 1366×800 on the knock page | Page re-laid; the browser test now plays that route at that size |
| "Or say it differently" could un-ink a made choice | The inked line stays while the page asks |
| "Turn back to Monday night" wiped the week with no warning | It now asks, and says what is kept and what goes |
| Phone: to-do card text ran off the page; Tomás's lines collided; ribbon covered a heading | Fixed |
| Friday's text covered Kit and the projector | Text moved to the sky |
| Options took over four seconds to appear | Writing speed raised by about a third |
| Mrs Leung's hand and Tomás's stammer too small | Enlarged |
| The last line gave no cue it could be typed | "write here" in pencil |
| The Asterwind token "reads like an advert" and does nothing | Yan now says she has not found the machine yet; still not interactive |

Not changed: action links that move after each press; the building labels' size; no Tuesday tab; Friday resolving in one paragraph when "one job each" is chosen with everything else in place. The last is a real criticism and is partly what the unresolved $340 and Saturday's margins are for.
