# Six Flights: wider review library

**All 18 review boards and four standalone surface studies.** This branch is a selection library, separate from the Request 11 production-candidate delivery. These are not approved game assets, and review-sheet cells are not individual high-resolution cutouts.

- [Cast and audience](cast/README.md): 6 boards with 132 labelled study panels: 60 named-cast rear studies, 48 audience studies and 24 contact-shadow shapes.
- [Environments](environment/README.md): 6 boards with 72 scene and lighting studies.
- [Props, paper and surfaces](props/README.md): 6 boards with 96 panels, plus 4 standalone surface studies.

That is **304 visual studies in 22 review image files**, not 304 production files. Different views or story states are distinguished in the source indexes. The same image in another format, prompts, references and ZIP packaging do not add to the count.

[Open every board and see actual options by scene or asset](REVIEW-INDEX.md).

## How to review

Choose by the printed candidate ID and the category's CSV index. The index's intended study may differ from the generated pose; judge the actual panel. Multiple options are included across all categories, not only the current shortlist. A suggested starting point is not an approval.

- Named-cast front/three-quarter production remains held pending the revised C01 gate. The new cast boards show rear studies only.
- The cast and environment boards include labels, cream gutters and small cells. Do not wire cropped cells straight into the game or upscale them and call them native 900px assets.
- Selected figures and props need standalone high-resolution transparent PNGs and a final dark-roof halo check. Selected environment directions need full-size standalone plates with newly measured screen/sheet/door rectangles.
- Grey shadow studies are not black-only runtime shadow files. Source-alpha masks may inform later black layers, but this delivery performs no such transformation.
- Surface candidates are not certified seamless; check the repeats in the final page layout. The masking-tape study retains genuine alpha.
- No new generation, scene integration, approval decision, app-code change, merge or deployment was performed as part of this GitHub delivery.

## Formats, provenance and originals

The source studies were created with built-in OpenAI image generation; model/version and seed were not exposed. Exact executed prompts and source indexes are kept in each category. Review WebPs retain native canvas dimensions, with no crop, upscale, recolour, relabelling or panel extraction. Category READMEs record the encoding and alpha checks. Original PNGs remain in the owner's original Library ZIPs; these WebPs are additional review derivatives, not replacements.

The source manifests describe the native PNG files and retain their original hash/dimension records. The delivery manifest separately identifies the committed WebP files. Source paths are mapping keys, not claims that a PNG is present in this branch.
