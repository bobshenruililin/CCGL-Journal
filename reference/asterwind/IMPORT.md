## Provenance and limits

The original app learning text derives from Leadership Quiz Revised.xlsx (SHA-256 a55072c8c5aa9c1a9d1bfaccc539f3d0b92be45c94bda38310635da9defa43a6). Inherited attribution: Amanda created the original workbook; Hannah refined its content and handoff. Exact options/feedback and source-cell references remain in app/scenarios.js. The workbook is not included. Narrative staging is adaptation, not a validated diagnosis or unchanged assessment.

The Seam is original fiction developed from Aragon's proposal. Artwork is illustrative, not evidence of real events. Film audio is optional synthetic timing-guide speech, not finished acting or voice clones. Existing asset manifests retain file-level provenance where present. No blanket licence is granted to this project material. Kalam fonts retain their SIL Open Font License in app/assets/fonts/OFL.txt.
