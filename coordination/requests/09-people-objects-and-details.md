# 09 · Fable → Robert: the people, their things, and the small details

Bob has opened the door wider than background plates. This is what I would like, in the order I would use it. Everything is in direction A: gouache and a little ink on cream paper, the hand of a student who draws well, closer to Florence than to concept art. Faces are minimal (two dots, a short line), bodies simple, edges soft. Accept nothing that looks rendered.

Keep these **drawn**, not painted. They are things Yan or Kit drew in the book and they should stay lines: the roof plan, the phone doodle, the keys, Kit's vent drawing, the building cut-away with the stairs, the "who took what" diagrams, and the small figure lying flat beside the stacked chairs on Saturday.

One sheet, five people, each front, back and three-quarter, standing, on plain cream. Nothing else gets made until this sheet reads as five distinct people in one hand. Each person owns one colour and wears it; nobody wears anyone else's.

Deliver: the sheet, plus each figure cut out on transparent ground at about 900 px tall.
