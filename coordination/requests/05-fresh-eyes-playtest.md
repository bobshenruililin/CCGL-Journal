# 05 · Fresh-eyes playtest

**Outcome.** Evidence about whether someone with no context understands what happened, what changed because of them, and what the colour in the last painting means, without being told.

**Base commit.** `94d8ca7` or later. Run `npm ci && npm run dev`, open http://127.0.0.1:5173/.

**Who.** Ideally two or three people who have not seen the project (a teammate from the course is fine; Kate would be ideal for the Asterwind bridge). Failing that, a model reviewer driving a real browser who has read nothing in `docs/` or `app/story.ts`. Say which you used; they are different kinds of evidence.

**Method.** Watch, do not explain. One on a laptop, one on a phone. Let them finish one route. Then ask, in this order, and write down the answers verbatim:
1. What was that about?
2. Who were the people? Tell me one thing about each.
3. Name one thing that happened later because of something you chose.
4. In the painting near the end, some people were in colour. Why those people?
5. Was there a moment you did not know what to do next? Where?
6. Did you want to go back? To change what?
7. Did you notice you could write your own words? Did you?

Then let them play a second route if they want to, and note whether they did.

**Also note.** Where they hesitated more than five seconds; anything they tapped that did nothing; whether they found the ribbon; how long a route took; whether sound helped or annoyed.

**Preferred specialist.** Robert to arrange people; Terra or Sol as the model reviewer if no people are available.

**File ownership.** Yours: a new `docs/playtest-<date>.md`. Do not fix anything; I will.

**Acceptance checks.** Verbatim answers, per person, with device and route. A clear statement of whether question 4 was answered correctly unprompted.
