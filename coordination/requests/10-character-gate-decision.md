**The hand is right and the five people are distinct. Approved as the style.** The soft edges, two-dot faces and one garment of colour each are what I hoped for. Go ahead now with anything seen **from behind** (request 09, section 2): faces do not show there, so nothing below blocks it.

Before any front or three-quarter poses, one revised sheet with these changes:

| Who | Keep | Change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Everyone | Minimal faces, soft gouache edge, dark trousers | They read as children. They are university students and one woman in her sixties. Lengthen the legs so adults stand about five heads tall, not three and a half. Keep the heads simple. |
| Yan | Bob, brown jacket, navy notebook held close | The pencil behind her ear is bright yellow, which is Tomás's colour. Make it plain wood or graphite grey. |
| Kit | Hood, round glasses, hands in the pocket | He is the same size and stance as Yan. Make him the slightest of the students: narrower, shoulders in, a little shorter, weight on one foot. The fringe should come down over the top of the glasses. |
| Priya | Bun, chin up, hand always mid-point | Gold hoop earrings read as marigold. Make them vermilion to match the jacket, or dark. |
| Tomás | Curls, scarf, forward lean | He should be the tallest and lankiest of the five. The wide-open laughing mouth in every view makes him a small child; give him a closed, lopsided smile in two of the three views. Arms can rest sometimes. |
| Mrs Leung | Perm, cardigan, folded arms, key on its cord | Nothing. She is exactly right. Keep her clearly the shortest and the only one who stands square. |

Height order, tallest first: Tomás, Priya, Yan, Kit, Mrs Leung.

One thing to hold on to in every pose that follows: people who have not been given anything to do are still whole people. Someone waiting should look like they are waiting for something, with their own weight and attention, never like a blank.
