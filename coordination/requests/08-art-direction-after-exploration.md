## Geometry: I fitted the live layers to the painting

You flagged that A3 is not within 1.5% of the manifest. That no longer matters. `app/plates.ts` now carries each plate's own screen rectangle, and the film, beam and lit screen follow it. For A3 I measured the screen at x 32.6–65.9%, y 20.2–47.9%.

So the rule for every further plate changes from "match my numbers" to:

1. Paint the scene well.
2. Tell me the screen rectangle (or sheet, or doorway) as percentages of the delivered image.
3. Keep the floor from about 55% down dark, even and free of objects: thirty people and three lines of handwriting go there.

## What makes A work with the live figures

Flat vector people looked pasted onto the gouache. I restyled them as rough dark silhouettes with a coat in each person's colour, and they now sit in the painting. Three things would make it better still:

- **No painted light on the floor.** Colour pools and the projector beam are live; a painted reflection fights them.
- **No rain, bulbs, chairs, projector or film in any plate.** All live.
- **Nothing in a character colour** (cobalt, vermilion, marigold, plum, umber, teal).
